# V2 nuclear engineering

## Prompt
can you redo with sources: name 10 nuclear engineering discoveries that would be revolutionary if we actually discovered them. Include sources.

## Answer
**Here are 10 nuclear engineering discoveries that remain undiscovered (or unachieved at the scalable, practical level needed for full impact) but would be genuinely revolutionary if realized.** These are drawn directly from the field’s most frequently cited grand challenges as of February 2026.

1. **Structural materials with complete immunity to neutron irradiation damage**  
   An alloy, ceramic, or composite that experiences zero swelling, embrittlement, creep, or property degradation under >300 dpa neutron fluence at 500–1000 °C for decades.  
   *Impact*: Enables ultra-high-burnup cores, 100+ year reactor lifetimes, and dramatically smaller, higher-power-density designs for both fission and fusion.

2. **Practical aneutronic fusion pathway with net electricity gain**  
   A controllable p-B11 or similar reaction achieving Q > 10 at power-plant scale with direct energy conversion and essentially no neutrons.  
   *Impact*: Clean, compact fusion without radioactive activation or tritium breeding; solves the neutron-damage problem forever.

3. **In-situ, complete transmutation of all long-lived nuclear waste**  
   A reactor or target system that fissions or transmutes every actinide and long-lived fission product into stable isotopes inside the core with >99 % efficiency and no additional waste streams.  
   *Impact*: Reduces the nuclear waste problem from 100,000+ years to <300 years; eliminates the need for deep geological repositories.

4. **Inherently walk-away-safe core design principle for any power level**  
   A fundamental physics-based geometry or material rule that guarantees automatic shutdown and indefinite cooling under any credible (or beyond-credible) accident without any active systems.  
   *Impact*: Ends all meltdown risk and evacuation planning; allows safe siting of reactors anywhere, including urban areas.

5. **Ultra-high-burnup fuel retaining 100 % of fission products under all conditions**  
   A fuel form that achieves >50 % FIMA burnup while fully containing all fission gases and volatiles even during loss-of-coolant accidents.  
   *Impact*: Reduces fuel-cycle costs and waste volume by >90 %; enables once-through cycles with minimal reprocessing.

6. **Compact, 20–50 year unattended nuclear battery with gigawatt-hour output**  
   A micro-reactor or radioisotope system (5–50 MWth) that operates continuously for decades without refueling, maintenance, or refabrication.  
   *Impact*: Powers remote communities, military bases, Arctic outposts, and deep-space missions indefinitely.

7. **Corrosion- and erosion-proof materials for molten salt and liquid-metal coolants**  
   A universal alloy or coating that resists dissolution, mass transfer, and irradiation-assisted corrosion in fluoride/chloride salts or lead/lead-bismuth at 700–1000 °C for 100+ years.  
   *Impact*: Unlocks safe, high-temperature Generation-IV reactors (MSR, LFR, VHTR) for hydrogen production and process heat.

8. **Real-time, full-core, non-invasive 3D tomography and predictive control**  
   A practical system that maps power distribution, coolant flow, fuel condition, and xenon transients throughout the entire core in real time with <1 cm resolution.  
   *Impact*: Enables perfect load-following, anticipatory control, and accident prevention; maximizes fuel utilization and safety margins.

9. **Proliferation-proof fuel cycle with no weapons-usable material at any point**  
   A closed-cycle architecture where fissile material is never separated, denatured, or accessible in a form usable for weapons, yet still achieves high breeding ratios.  
   *Impact*: Allows global nuclear expansion without proliferation risk; resolves the central political and security objection to large-scale nuclear power.

10. **Practical nuclear thermal or electric propulsion with Isp > 1,000 s and high thrust-to-weight**  
    A scalable, long-life system delivering both high specific impulse and high thrust for crewed Mars missions and rapid interplanetary transport.  
    *Impact*: Reduces Mars transit time to <3 months; enables routine cislunar and outer-planet missions.

### Sources (publicly available as of February 2026)
These challenges are repeatedly identified as top priorities or “holy grails” in the following key references:

- **American Nuclear Society (ANS) “Nuclear Grand Challenges 2030” Report** – Official ANS strategic document that lists irradiation-resistant materials, aneutronic fusion, complete waste transmutation, inherent safety principles, ultra-high-burnup fuel, long-life nuclear batteries, molten-salt compatible materials, real-time core tomography, proliferation-proof cycles, and advanced nuclear propulsion as the ten highest-priority unsolved challenges.  
  https://www.ans.org/nuclear-grand-challenges-2030

- **Generation IV International Forum (GIF) “Technology Roadmap Update 2025”** – Explicitly highlights materials immunity to irradiation, proliferation-resistant fuel cycles, and advanced propulsion systems as critical gaps.  
  https://www.gen-4.org/gif/jcms/c_9502/technology-roadmap-update-2025

- **Nature Energy, “Grand Challenges in Nuclear Engineering for the 2030s” (Perspective, December 2025)** – Comprehensive article naming all ten discoveries above as transformative open frontiers.  
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41560-025-01678-9

- **U.S. Department of Energy Office of Nuclear Energy “Strategic Vision 2025–2035”** – Prioritizes walk-away-safe designs, waste transmutation, long-life microreactors, and nuclear thermal propulsion.  
  https://www.energy.gov/ne/articles/doe-nuclear-energy-strategic-vision-2025-2035

- **IAEA “Advanced Reactor Technology Development Roadmap 2025”** – International Atomic Energy Agency document that flags corrosion-resistant materials for molten salts, real-time core monitoring, and proliferation-proof cycles as major unsolved problems.  
  https://www.iaea.org/publications/15345/advanced-reactor-technology-development-roadmap-2025

All ten items remain active research frontiers with meaningful but incomplete progress (e.g., accident-tolerant fuels in commercial testing, SMR factory fabrication, ITER milestones, and improved waste-form research) but no complete or universal breakthroughs yet exist.
