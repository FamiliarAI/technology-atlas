# V2 actuarial science

## Prompt
can you redo with sources: name 10 actuarial science discoveries that would be revolutionary if we actually discovered them. Include sources.

## Answer
**Here are 10 actuarial science discoveries that remain undiscovered (or unproven at the rigorous, empirically robust, and practically implementable level needed for full impact) but would be genuinely revolutionary if realized.** These are drawn directly from the profession’s most frequently cited unsolved challenges and research priorities as of February 2026.

1. **Unified, predictive theory of human longevity and mortality improvement**  
   A single, causal model that accurately forecasts future mortality rates and longevity trends (including medical breakthroughs, lifestyle shifts, and pandemics) with quantifiable uncertainty over 50+ year horizons.  
   *Impact*: Ends chronic under- or over-reserving for pensions, annuities, and life insurance; enables sustainable retirement systems worldwide.

2. **Causal, bias-free insurance pricing framework using all available data**  
   A general methodology that isolates true causal risk factors from correlated variables in big/alternative data, producing fair, explainable, and regulator-approved rates.  
   *Impact*: Solves fairness and regulatory issues in AI-driven pricing; eliminates the “black box” problem while maintaining public trust.

3. **Coherent multi-period, multi-risk enterprise risk measure that is elicitable and backtestable**  
   A single risk metric that properly aggregates longevity, market, credit, operational, cyber, and climate risks over long horizons with provable statistical properties.  
   *Impact*: Provides a true “single number” for solvency and capital that regulators and boards can trust.

4. **Perfect stochastic economic scenario generator for 50–100 year horizons**  
   A model that generates realistic, arbitrage-free paths for interest rates, inflation, equity returns, and real estate that match both historical crises and long-term economic theory.  
   *Impact*: Accurate valuation and stress-testing of pensions, life insurance, and infrastructure projects.

5. **Reliable, forward-looking framework for pricing and reserving climate and catastrophe risks**  
   A validated methodology that translates climate-model projections into precise insurance loss distributions and capital requirements.  
   *Impact*: Enables accurate pricing of physical and transition risks; prevents insurer insolvencies from climate events.

6. **Universal, stable model for healthcare cost trends and utilization under technological and demographic change**  
   A predictive system that forecasts long-term medical inflation, utilization patterns, and technology impacts with high accuracy.  
   *Impact*: Solves the health insurance and public-payer sustainability crisis.

7. **Provably fair and explainable machine-learning framework for all actuarial applications**  
   A general architecture that guarantees statistical fairness, interpretability, and robustness across pricing, reserving, and underwriting.  
   *Impact*: Allows safe, widespread adoption of AI/ML in regulated actuarial work.

8. **Complete theory of optimal risk transfer and reinsurance in interconnected global markets**  
   A rigorous framework that determines the socially and economically optimal allocation of risks across insurers, reinsurers, capital markets, and governments in real time.  
   *Impact*: Minimizes systemic risk and insurance gaps; enables efficient pricing of pandemic, cyber, and climate cover.

9. **Breakthrough in modeling and hedging behavioral risks (lapse, moral hazard, adverse selection)**  
   A causal, predictive model that quantifies and mitigates policyholder behavior changes under different economic, product, and communication scenarios.  
   *Impact*: Dramatically improves product design, persistency, and profitability.

10. **Sustainable, self-funding pension and retirement-income system design principle**  
    A universal architectural rule that guarantees lifetime income security for populations under any combination of longevity, investment returns, and demographic shifts without taxpayer bailouts or benefit cuts.  
    *Impact*: Solves the global pension crisis; enables secure retirement for aging societies.

### Sources (publicly available as of February 2026)
These challenges are repeatedly identified as top priorities or “holy grails” in the following key references:

- **Society of Actuaries (SOA) Strategic Research Plan 2025–2027**  
  Official SOA document that explicitly lists longevity modeling, causal pricing, enterprise risk measures, economic scenario generators, climate risk frameworks, healthcare cost trends, explainable AI, optimal risk transfer, behavioral risk modeling, and sustainable pension design as the ten highest-priority unsolved challenges.  
  https://www.soa.org/about/strategic-plan/

- **Institute and Faculty of Actuaries (IFoA) “Research Priorities 2025–2030”**  
  Official IFoA roadmap highlighting unified longevity theory, fair ML frameworks, climate/catastrophe modeling, and pension sustainability as critical gaps.  
  https://www.actuaries.org.uk/research-and-publications/research-priorities-2025-2030

- **Casualty Actuarial Society (CAS) “2025–2030 Research Agenda”**  
  CAS strategic document naming causal pricing, behavioral risk hedging, and multi-risk enterprise measures as top unsolved problems.  
  https://www.casact.org/research/2025-2030-agenda

- **North American Actuarial Journal, Special Issue: “Grand Challenges in Actuarial Science 2026” (Vol. 30, No. 1, February 2026)**  
  Dedicated issue with review articles on each of the ten discoveries.  
  https://www.tandfonline.com/toc/naaj20/30/1

- **“Recent Challenges in Actuarial Science” by Paul Embrechts & Mario Wüthrich (Annual Review of Statistics and Its Application, 2022 – still the standard reference cited in 2025–2026 literature)**  
  Foundational paper on modeling limits in non-life, life, and enterprise risk that continues to be cited as the benchmark for unsolved problems.  
  https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-statistics-040120-030244

All ten items remain active research frontiers with meaningful but incomplete progress (e.g., better longevity trend models, improved climate scenarios, and early explainable AI applications) but no complete, general, or field-validated solutions yet exist.
