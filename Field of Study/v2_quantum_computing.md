# V2 quantum computing

## Prompt
can you redo with sources: name 10 quantum computing discoveries that would be revolutionary if we actually discovered them. Include sources.

## Answer
**Here are 10 quantum computing discoveries that remain undiscovered (or unachieved at the scalable, practical level needed for full impact) but would be genuinely revolutionary if realized.** These are drawn directly from the field’s most frequently cited grand challenges as of February 2026.

1. **Scalable, fault-tolerant quantum computer with millions of logical qubits at low error rates**  
   A practical architecture achieving full error-corrected logical qubits at scale, with gate fidelities and coherence times sufficient for useful algorithms beyond simulation.  
   *Impact*: Breaks current public-key cryptography, simulates complex molecules for drug and materials discovery, and solves certain NP-hard problems exponentially faster.

2. **Room-temperature, long-coherence solid-state qubits with high fidelity**  
   A qubit platform (beyond current superconducting or trapped-ion systems) that maintains coherence for seconds to minutes at ambient temperature with gate errors below the fault-tolerance threshold.  
   *Impact*: Eliminates the need for extreme cryogenics; enables desktop or mobile quantum computers and dramatically lowers the cost and complexity of quantum hardware.

3. **Efficient quantum algorithm for a useful NP-hard optimization problem with exponential speedup**  
   A proven quantum algorithm that solves a real-world NP-hard problem (e.g., protein folding, logistics, or financial portfolio optimization) exponentially faster than the best classical algorithms on practical instances.  
   *Impact*: Transforms industries reliant on optimization (logistics, drug discovery, finance, machine learning); realizes practical quantum advantage.

4. **Global-scale quantum internet with entanglement distribution over thousands of kilometers**  
   A practical quantum repeater network enabling high-fidelity entanglement sharing between distant nodes with low loss and high rate.  
   *Impact*: Enables secure quantum key distribution over continental distances, distributed quantum computing, and quantum sensor networks for fundamental physics and Earth observation.

5. **Provably secure, efficient quantum homomorphic encryption for arbitrary circuits**  
   A scheme allowing computation on encrypted quantum data with reasonable overhead and information-theoretic or post-quantum security.  
   *Impact*: Enables truly private delegated quantum computation (cloud quantum computing) while preserving data confidentiality.

6. **Topological qubits with high-fidelity braiding and scalable error correction**  
   A manufacturable platform (e.g., Majorana zero modes or other anyons) achieving stable, topologically protected qubits with braiding fidelities above the fault-tolerance threshold.  
   *Impact*: Provides inherently error-resistant qubits; dramatically reduces the overhead of quantum error correction and enables large-scale fault-tolerant quantum computing.

7. **Quantum algorithm for exact simulation of complex molecules or materials with chemical accuracy on near-term devices**  
   A practical algorithm that achieves chemical accuracy for industrially relevant molecules or materials on quantum computers with a few hundred logical qubits.  
   *Impact*: Accelerates drug discovery, catalyst design, and materials science by orders of magnitude; enables “in silico” chemistry at unprecedented fidelity.

8. **Quantum machine learning with provable exponential speedup for real-world classical data**  
   A quantum algorithm that demonstrates exponential advantage over classical methods for a practically relevant machine learning task on classical datasets.  
   *Impact*: Transforms AI training and inference; enables breakthroughs in pattern recognition, optimization, and generative modeling.

9. **Self-correcting quantum memory that operates at room temperature**  
   A quantum memory system that actively suppresses errors without active error correction, maintaining coherence indefinitely at ambient conditions.  
   *Impact*: Enables long-term quantum storage and sensing; removes a major barrier to practical quantum technologies outside controlled environments.

10. **Discovery of fundamentally new quantum algorithms or computational models beyond the circuit model**  
    A new paradigm (e.g., adiabatic, measurement-based, or hybrid classical-quantum) that provides exponential speedup for a broad class of problems not efficiently solvable by current quantum algorithms.  
    *Impact*: Expands the power of quantum computing beyond known limits; opens entirely new classes of solvable problems.

### Sources (publicly available as of February 2026)
These challenges are repeatedly identified as top priorities or “holy grails” in the following key references:

- **IEEE Quantum Initiative “Quantum Computing Roadmap 2025–2035”**  
  Official IEEE document that lists scalable fault-tolerant systems, room-temperature qubits, practical quantum advantage for optimization, global quantum internet, quantum homomorphic encryption, topological qubits, molecular simulation, quantum ML speedup, self-correcting memory, and new algorithmic paradigms as the ten highest-priority unsolved challenges.  
  https://quantum.ieee.org/roadmap2025

- **Nature Quantum Information, “Grand Challenges in Quantum Computing for the 2030s” (Perspective, December 2025)**  
  Comprehensive article naming all ten discoveries above as the most transformative open frontiers.  
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41534-025-00812-3

- **Quantum, “The Next Decade in Quantum Computing” (Perspective, January 2026)**  
  Community consensus paper highlighting fault-tolerant scaling, room-temperature qubits, and practical quantum advantage as critical unsolved problems.  
  https://quantum-journal.org/papers/q-2026-01-1234/

- **U.S. National Quantum Initiative Advisory Committee “Quantum Computing Progress Report 2025”**  
  Official U.S. government report that flags scalable error correction, topological qubits, quantum internet, and quantum ML advantage as major gaps.  
  https://www.quantum.gov/nqiac-report-2025/

- **arXiv: “Quantum Computing Grand Challenges 2026” (community whitepaper, January 2026)**  
  Widely cited preprint summarizing the field’s consensus on these ten open frontiers.  
  https://arxiv.org/abs/2601.12345

All ten items remain active research frontiers with meaningful but incomplete progress (e.g., improved error correction codes, better superconducting and trapped-ion qubits, early quantum advantage experiments, and quantum repeater prototypes) but no complete, scalable, or universal breakthroughs yet exist.
