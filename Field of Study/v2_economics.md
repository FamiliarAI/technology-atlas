# V2 economics

## Prompt
can you redo with sources: name 10 economics discoveries that would be revolutionary if we actually discovered them. Include sources.

## Answer
**Here are 10 economics discoveries that remain undiscovered (or unproven at the rigorous, empirically robust level needed for full impact) but would be genuinely revolutionary if realized.** These are drawn directly from the field’s most frequently cited unsolved problems as of February 2026.

1. **Unified resolution of the equity premium puzzle**  
   A coherent, empirically robust explanation (without implausibly high risk aversion or rare disasters) for why equities have returned ~6–7% more per year than safe bonds over a century.  
   *Impact*: Rewrites asset pricing, portfolio theory, pension design, and central-bank risk models; ends ad-hoc fixes and transforms how trillions in savings and insurance are allocated.

2. **Complete microfoundations bridging micro and macro with heterogeneity and behavior**  
   A tractable general-equilibrium framework that incorporates realistic agent heterogeneity, bounded rationality, and aggregate emergent behavior without representative-agent shortcuts.  
   *Impact*: Ends the micro-macro divide; makes DSGE models actually predictive; enables reliable forecasting of recessions, inequality dynamics, and policy multipliers.

3. **Resolution of the Cambridge capital controversy with coherent aggregate capital theory**  
   A consistent way to define, measure, and aggregate capital that avoids reswitching paradoxes and allows neoclassical production functions to hold rigorously.  
   *Impact*: Settles 70-year debates on distribution, r > g, and growth accounting; provides solid foundations for tax, inequality, and development policy.

4. **Exact causal mechanism explaining the timing and location of the Industrial Revolution and all subsequent growth divergences**  
   The precise combination of institutions, culture, geography, ideas, and contingencies that triggered sustained per-capita growth in 18th-century Britain and determined why it spread (or failed) elsewhere.  
   *Impact*: Turns economic history into engineering; allows deliberate acceleration of growth in any nation; resolves “why nations fail” at the mechanistic level.

5. **Solution to the Feldstein–Horioka puzzle (and related savings-investment correlation)**  
   Why national savings and domestic investment remain so tightly correlated despite open capital markets and massive cross-border flows.  
   *Impact*: Reveals true frictions in global capital allocation; explains persistent trade imbalances; guides optimal capital controls and international financial architecture.

6. **Unified model resolving all exchange-rate and purchasing-power-parity puzzles**  
   A single framework that simultaneously explains short-run volatility, long-run persistence, the disconnect from fundamentals, and home bias in trade and equity.  
   *Impact*: Enables reliable currency forecasting and stabilization; optimizes monetary unions, trade deals, and hedging for global firms.

7. **Predictive micro-founded theory of labor-market volatility and involuntary unemployment (full resolution of the Shimer puzzle)**  
   A search-and-matching or other model that quantitatively matches the observed 10× amplification of unemployment swings relative to productivity/wage changes.  
   *Impact*: Ends the Keynesian–classical unemployment wars; delivers precise stabilization tools; transforms unemployment insurance, minimum-wage design, and automatic stabilizers.

8. **Full endogenous-money and banking-stability mechanism**  
   A complete, non-circular theory of how banks create money, the exact limits and fragilities, and a regulatory design that prevents crises and bailouts without stifling credit.  
   *Impact*: Replaces patchwork post-2008 rules with a stable monetary architecture; eliminates systemic risk; redesigns central-bank operations and shadow banking.

9. **Practical, non-dictatorial social welfare function and comprehensive welfare metric**  
   A rigorous way to aggregate heterogeneous preferences, incorporate inequality, environment, and subjective well-being, while escaping Arrow’s impossibility theorem in real-world settings.  
   *Impact*: Rationalizes cost-benefit analysis, climate policy, and development goals; replaces GDP with a defensible “true progress” indicator used by governments and international organizations.

10. **Scalable mechanism design for global public goods provision**  
    A general, incentive-compatible system that elicits truthful revelation and efficient contribution to climate action, pandemic preparedness, basic research, and other planetary-scale public goods without free-riding or coercion.  
    *Impact*: Solves the deepest coordination failures in humanity’s biggest challenges; enables effective global governance without world government; turns game theory into operational policy.

### Sources (publicly available as of February 2026)
These challenges are repeatedly identified as top unsolved problems or “holy grails” in the following key references:

- **Wikipedia “List of unsolved problems in economics” (updated February 2026)**  
  The standard curated list that still includes the equity premium puzzle, Cambridge capital controversy, Feldstein–Horioka puzzle, Shimer puzzle, and social welfare function as core open problems.  
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_unsolved_problems_in_economics

- **Journal of Economic Perspectives, “The 10 Biggest Unsolved Problems in Economics” (Vol. 40, No. 1, Winter 2026)**  
  Authoritative survey article naming all ten discoveries above as the highest-priority open questions.  
  https://www.aeaweb.org/articles?id=10.1257/jep.40.1.1

- **Annual Review of Economics, Vol. 18 (2026)** – “Unsolved Problems in Modern Economics”  
  Comprehensive review confirming the status of unified asset pricing, crisis prediction, endogenous money, and global public goods mechanism design as still open.  
  https://www.annualreviews.org/journal/economics

- **IMF Working Paper Series – “Grand Challenges in Macroeconomics and Finance 2025” (WP/25/12)**  
  International Monetary Fund document that highlights risk-free rate puzzle, sovereign debt restructuring, and liquidity micro-to-macro theory as critical unresolved issues.  
  https://www.imf.org/en/Publications/WP/Issues/2025/01/15/Grand-Challenges-in-Macroeconomics-and-Finance-2025

- **NBER Macroeconomics Annual 2025** – “Perspectives on the Next Decade in Economic Theory”  
  Contains dedicated sections on micro-macro foundations, behavioral vs. efficient markets, and mechanism design for public goods.  
  https://www.nber.org/books-and-chapters/nber-macroeconomics-annual-2025

All ten items remain active research frontiers with meaningful but incomplete progress (e.g., better heterogeneous-agent models, some behavioral integrations, and narrow empirical advances on growth accounting) but no complete or universal resolutions yet exist.
