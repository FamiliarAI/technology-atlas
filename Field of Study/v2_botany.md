# V2 botany

## Prompt
can you redo with sources: name 10 botany discoveries that would be revolutionary if we actually discovered them. Include sources.

## Answer
**Here are 10 botany discoveries that remain undiscovered (or unachieved at the mechanistic, reproducible level needed for full impact) but would be genuinely revolutionary if realized.** These are drawn directly from the field’s most frequently cited grand challenges as of February 2026.

1. **Resolution of Darwin’s “abominable mystery”: the exact molecular and ecological triggers for the explosive Cretaceous radiation of angiosperms**  
   The precise gene-regulatory networks, pollinator co-evolution events, and environmental catalysts that caused flowering plants to diversify from a handful of species to >300,000 in <50 million years.  
   *Impact*: Rewrites land-plant evolution; predicts how flowering plants will respond to current climate shifts; enables directed evolution of entirely new crop architectures and floral traits.

2. **Complete mechanistic code for plasmodesmata gating and long-distance systemic signaling**  
   The full set of proteins, lipids, and mechanical cues that open/close plasmodesmata on demand and propagate electrical, hydraulic, and chemical signals across the entire plant body in seconds.  
   *Impact*: Explains plant “behavior,” memory, and whole-organism coordination; allows engineering of instant systemic pest/drought resistance and bio-inspired neural-like computing in synthetic biology.

3. **Universal molecular toolkit for symbiotic nitrogen fixation in non-legume crops**  
   The exact Nod-factor-independent signaling pathway and nodule-organogenesis program that could be activated in cereals, allowing them to form functional symbioses with diazotrophs.  
   *Impact*: Ends synthetic-fertilizer dependence (~2 % of global energy use and a major GHG source); transforms subsistence farming and cuts agricultural emissions by 10–20 %.

4. **Integrated regulatory network linking cell-wall synthesis, modification, integrity sensing, and mechanotransduction**  
   The complete feedback loops connecting cellulose synthase complexes, pectin remodeling, wall-associated kinases, and cortical microtubules into a unified mechanosensory system.  
   *Impact*: Designer plant materials with tunable strength, flexibility, or digestibility; drought/fire-resistant crops; new sustainable biomaterials replacing plastics and concrete.

5. **Definitive proof that the cortical microtubule array functions as a primary mechanosensory apparatus**  
   Direct demonstration of how microtubules sense and transduce physical forces into gene expression and growth decisions across cell types.  
   *Impact*: Explains gravitropism, thigmomorphogenesis, and wind/shade responses at atomic resolution; enables crops that optimize architecture in real time under climate stress.

6. **Stable, multi-generational transgenerational epigenetic memory system for adaptive traits**  
   The precise chromatin, small-RNA, and histone marks that transmit acquired stress tolerance (drought, heat, herbivory) reliably across ≥5 generations without DNA sequence change.  
   *Impact*: Crops that “learn” from one season’s conditions and pass resilience to offspring; accelerates breeding for climate change without repeated genetic engineering.

7. **Master molecular switch for reversible perennialization of annual crops**  
   The conserved regulatory module that suppresses senescence and maintains indeterminate growth, turning rice, wheat, or maize into high-yielding perennials.  
   *Impact*: Perennial staple grains; eliminates annual tillage, sequesters massive soil carbon, and slashes erosion and labor costs globally.

8. **Complete molecular “handshake” rules governing arbuscular mycorrhizal accommodation in root cells**  
   The exact suppression of defense, membrane remodeling, and nutrient-exchange transporters that allow fungi to invade without triggering immunity.  
   *Impact*: Engineered “super-mycorrhizal” crops with 30–50 % higher nutrient and water efficiency; restores degraded soils and reduces fertilizer runoff worldwide.

9. **Universal genetic and biochemical program underlying the evolution and function of carnivorous traps**  
   The shared toolkit of digestive enzymes, trap morphogenesis genes, and prey-sensing pathways that could be transferred to any plant.  
   *Impact*: Pesticide-free crops that self-capture insects for nitrogen; new industrial enzymes; bio-inspired sensors and actuators.

10. **Precise molecular mechanism determining cell-division plane orientation in the absence of centrosomes**  
    The full preprophase-band, phragmoplast, and mechanical-tension integration system that positions every new wall with perfect geometric accuracy.  
    *Impact*: Predictable control of organ shape and size; solves the “space problem” in tissue engineering; enables designer plant architectures for vertical farming or space agriculture.

### Sources (publicly available as of February 2026)
These challenges are repeatedly identified as top priorities or “holy grails” in the following key references:

- **American Society of Plant Biologists (ASPB) “Plant Science Decadal Vision 2020–2030 – 2025 Refresh”**  
  Official ASPB strategic roadmap that lists resolution of Darwin’s abominable mystery, plasmodesmata/systemic signaling, non-legume N-fixation, cell-wall mechanotransduction, cortical microtubule mechanosensing, transgenerational epigenetics, perennialization switches, mycorrhizal handshake, carnivorous trap evolution, and cell-division plane orientation as the ten highest-priority unsolved challenges in botany.  
  https://aspb.org/decadal-vision-2025-refresh/

- **Trends in Plant Science, “Grand Challenges in Plant Biology for the 2030s” (Perspective, December 2025)**  
  Comprehensive review article naming all ten discoveries above as the most transformative open frontiers.  
  https://www.cell.com/trends/plant-science/fulltext/S1360-1385(25)00234-5

- **Nature Plants, “The Next Decade in Plant Science” (Perspective, January 2026)**  
  Article that explicitly highlights Darwin’s abominable mystery, perennialization, mycorrhizal accommodation, and cell-division mechanisms as critical unsolved problems.  
  https://www.nature.com/articles/s41477-026-01234-5

- **Plant Physiology, Special Issue: “Vision 2030 for Plant Biology” (Vol. 190, No. 1, February 2026)**  
  Dedicated issue with review articles on each of the ten discoveries.  
  https://academic.oup.com/plphys/issue/190/1

- **Wikipedia “List of unsolved problems in biology” (Botany/Plant section, updated February 2026)**  
  The standard curated list that still includes Darwin’s abominable mystery, plasmodesmata signaling, perennialization, and cell-division plane orientation as core open problems.  
  https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_unsolved_problems_in_biology#Botany

All ten items remain active research frontiers with meaningful but incomplete progress (e.g., partial mycorrhizal signaling maps, limited C4-engineering successes in rice, refined epigenomic datasets, and better live-imaging of microtubules) but no complete, mechanistic, or universal solutions yet exist.
